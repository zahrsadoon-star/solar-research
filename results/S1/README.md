## What was run and saved

`scripts/s1_eda.py --outage-only` reads the analysis workbook's `Hourly Data`,
compares labels with the HOMER workbook's `National Status`, checks individual
events and summary statistics against `Outage Events` (header on Excel row 3),
and fits a descriptive Weibull by maximum likelihood to complete event durations.
The loader docstrings list exact source columns. Raw files are read-only.

Outputs in `eda/`:

- `summary.json`: source hashes, software versions, configuration, hourly/monthly
  summaries, label and clock disagreements, event comparisons, duration fit.
- `tables.csv`: monthly/hourly/joint denominators and shares, events, duration
  histogram and fitted density underlying the figures.
- `outage_hour_month.png`, `outage_duration.png`: 300 dpi descriptive figures.

No temperature figure or daily temperature table was generated.

## Findings and target policy

Values below come from `eda/summary.json`, generated by the script. The 8,760
hourly rows contain 4,324 positive-generator proxy hours (49.3607%) in 1,064
events. Mean duration is 4.06391 h, median 3 h, range 1–59 h, sample standard
deviation 3.74714 h, and linearly interpolated 95th percentile 10.85 h.
All workbook event records and summary statistics agree (zero differences).
No event touches a year boundary. The continuous Weibull approximation uses
1,064 events, shape 1.39082 and scale 4.51927 h, with location fixed at zero.
This fit does not establish a Weibull data-generating distribution.

The workbook explicitly defines outage as positive generator output. Interpret
the target as **generator operation, a proxy for national-grid unavailability**.
Generator agreement is 100%, an internal consistency check, not independent
validation. Conditional on row-order alignment, National Status agreement is
99.95434% with full comparator coverage and four disagreements. These occur at
2018-07-21 20:00 and 21:00, and 2018-07-22 06:00 and 10:00 Baghdad time: status
is OFF while the source outage label and both energy sources are zero.
Retain these source labels and expose the conflicting records; do not silently
relabel or claim independent grid verification. Adjudication requires independent
evidence from the lead/data owner.

The status workbook has 745 usable full datetimes and 8,015 time-only values.
Clock auditing finds 7,682 mismatches and 333 matches among time-only values;
all observed time-only values are midnight. Matching rowwise grid energy and
full datetimes support a positional comparison but do not independently confirm
the missing calendar dates or the inconsistent clocks. Every clock mismatch is
saved in the JSON; the alignment is explicitly conditional.

## Weaknesses and next work

Annual timestamps were reconstructed from row order, with only partial January
validation. One household and one year limit generalization. Labels and event
references share provenance. Hourly durations are discrete and serially
dependent. No forecasting performance, significance, causal temperature effect
or dispatch benefit has been established. Full-year EDA has been inspected;
later evaluation months cannot be described as wholly unseen.

Completed-event durations, future generator/grid energy, realized daily maximum
temperature and full-year climatology must not enter earlier forecasts. A future
daily temperature scatter would show descriptive association only. Before WP2,
freeze tuning budgets, search spaces, selection rules, baseline fallbacks,
ablations and dependence-aware inference. The present task does not select these
future protocol choices.
