## B. Iraqi calendar 2018 (task 3)

General caveat: in Iraq the start of Islamic months is set separately by the Sunni Endowment (Diwan al-Waqf al-Sunni), the office of Grand Ayatollah al-Sistani (followed by most Shia, i.e. the majority in Baghdad), and other Najaf marjas; they often differ by one day. The federal cabinet then declares the public-holiday days. For an hourly Baghdad load/outage model, the government holiday days matter more than the religious first day.
