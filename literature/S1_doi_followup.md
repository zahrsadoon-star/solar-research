### Effect on the S1 novelty claim
- "First to use outage foresight for household battery dispatch in Iraq" is **no longer defensible**: Mohammed et al. (2026, Energies) already do household battery scheduling under Iraqi outages with a perfect-foresight benchmark, and the same group's SSRN preprint may already pair a forecaster with scheduling (content unverified).
- Twala et al. (2023) already feed a load-shedding (stage) forecast into household PV-battery scheduling in South Africa.
- Still defensible (pending a read of the SSRN preprint): (i) a calibrated, probabilistic forecaster trained on a measured *household-level* Iraqi supply record with time-ordered evaluation against persistence/Markov baselines (DM/HLN tests); (ii) an explicit value-of-forecast decomposition: no-information vs always-full reserve vs forecast-driven vs perfect foresight, reported as % of perfect-foresight value captured; (iii) inclusion of a calendar-ageing cost so that "keep the battery full" is not free.
- Action for the lead: obtain and read SSRN 6715504/6793802 before finalising the novelty paragraph.
